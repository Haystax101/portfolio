Supercharged

Find internships through the people already there.

Cold outreach is how students actually land spring weeks and graduate roles in finance, trading and consulting, and almost nobody does it well. Tell Supercharged the role, the firms and the season; it finds the alumni and analysts already there, scores who will actually reply across five compatibility dimensions, and drafts the email in your voice. Then it tracks the whole pipeline.

  • Natural-language people discovery over a structured talent graph
  • Outreach drafting, reply tracking and a pipeline view, in one app
  • Launched to a pilot cohort at Oxford; waitlist live at superchargedai.app

Supercharged: Grow Together (v1, codename Scrollwise)

The feed you're addicted to, pointed at things worth reading.

A React Native app that keeps the mechanics of a short-form feed but fills it with long-form ideas: articles, podcasts and books, surfaced by search rather than an engagement optimiser. Released on the iOS App Store in September 2025 as the first product under the Supercharged name.

  • Hybrid retrieval: Gemini embeddings in pgvector and Postgres full-text search, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion
  • Progressive search returns lexical hits instantly, then re-ranks once the embedding round-trip resolves
  • 13 Deno edge functions, roughly 160 components, cron-batched push notifications with receipt pruning
  • React Native
  • Expo
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Postgres
  • pgvector
  • Deno
  • EAS

Get There One Day

A community for ambitious young people, built around a 100% Q&A podcast.

Started as a podcast answering the questions degree apprentices and school leavers actually ask, and grew past 14,000 followers on TikTok.
